This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our online store, why we collect it, and what rights you have. It applies to the storefront, checkout and customer accounts.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is {{seller.name}}, {{seller.address}}, tax identification number {{seller.taxId}}.

For anything about your data, write to {{seller.email}} or call {{seller.phone}}.

## 2. What data we collect

- **At checkout:** your email address, full name, delivery address and, if you give it, your phone number. We need these to deliver your order and to send you emails about it.
- **Your account (optional):** your email address, name, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), saved delivery addresses and your order history.
- **Your cart:** the products you add. A cart is linked to your browser by a cookie and is kept for 30 days after its last change.
- **Orders:** what you bought, prices, the delivery fee, any promo code, the version of the terms of sale you accepted and when, and the payment status. Card details are entered on Stripe's page; we never see or store your full card number.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

- **To take, deliver and support your order** (contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): checkout data, order emails, complaints and returns.
- **To run your account** (contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), if you create one.
- **To keep accounting and tax records** (legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): paid orders and invoices.
- **To prevent fraud and keep the store secure** (our legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): for example, checking payments and protecting accounts.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for automated decisions with legal effects.

## 4. Who processes your data for us

We use these service providers. Each processes data only on our instructions and under a data processing agreement.

- **Supabase:** database hosting and customer account sign in.
- **Vercel:** hosting of the website.
- **Stripe:** card payments. Stripe is also an independent controller for some payment data, under its own privacy policy.
- **Resend:** sending order and account emails.
- **Delivery carriers:** [[TO FILL: the carriers that receive delivery addresses]], to deliver your parcel.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Checkouts that were never paid:** the email, name, address and phone are deleted 30 days after the checkout expired. What remains is anonymous.
- **Your account:** until you delete it. You can delete it at any time in your account settings; your profile and saved addresses are removed.
- **Paid orders:** for as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually 5 years from the end of the year the tax was due, and for as long as a claim about the order could still be made.
- **Your cart:** 30 days after its last change.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data and get a copy of it,
- have inaccurate data corrected,
- have your data deleted, where we no longer need it or the law allows,
- restrict how we use it,
- receive your data in a portable format,
- object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

To use any of these rights, write to {{seller.email}}. We answer within one month.

## 7. Your right to complain

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, [https://uodo.gov.pl](https://uodo.gov.pl).

## 8. Cookies

We use only the cookies the store needs to work, such as the one that keeps your cart. The [cookie policy](/cookies) lists every cookie and lets you change your choice.

## 9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
