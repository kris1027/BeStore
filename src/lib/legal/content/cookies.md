## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for the site to work. Others are optional, and we only set them if you agree.

## The three kinds we may use

- **Necessary:** they make the store work, for example keeping your cart and your sign in. They are always on, because the store cannot work without them.
- **Analytics:** they would help us understand how the store is used, so we can improve it.
- **Marketing:** they would help show you relevant offers on other sites.

Right now we use necessary cookies only. If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, we will ask you first and list them below.

## Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings button on this page or in the footer of every page. Saying no to optional cookies is as easy as saying yes, and the store works the same either way.

## Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a no to analytics and marketing cookies, unless you later turn them on yourself in Cookie settings.
