If you are a consumer, you can withdraw from a purchase within 14 days, without giving any reason. This page explains how.

## 1. How long you have

You have 14 days from the day you, or someone you name (other than the carrier), receive the goods. If one order arrives in several parcels, the 14 days run from the day the last parcel arrives.

## 2. How to tell us

Before the 14 days end, send us a clear statement that you are withdrawing. You can:

- email {{seller.email}}, or
- send a letter to {{seller.name}}, {{seller.address}}.

You can use the model withdrawal form at the bottom of this page, but you do not have to. Please include your order number. Sending your statement before the deadline is enough. We confirm that we received it by email.

## 3. Sending the goods back

Send the goods back to {{seller.address}} within 14 days of telling us you withdraw. Sending them before the 14 days end is enough.

You pay the cost of sending the goods back, unless we agreed otherwise. Please pack the goods safely. You are responsible for any loss in value caused by handling the goods beyond what is needed to check what they are, how they work and their features, as you could in a shop.

## 4. Your refund

We refund everything you paid for the goods, including the cheapest standard delivery fee, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal statement. We refund to the card you paid with; you pay no fee for the refund. Your bank usually shows the refund within 5 to 10 business days.

We may wait to refund until we receive the goods back, or until you show us proof that you sent them, whichever comes first.

## 5. When the right does not apply

The right of withdrawal does not apply, for example, to:

- goods made to your own specification or clearly personalised,
- goods that spoil quickly,
- sealed goods that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed after delivery,

## 6. Faulty goods

If the goods are faulty or not as described, you also have rights under the statutory warranty, which are separate from this right of withdrawal. See section 9 of the [terms of sale](/terms).
