You can buy as a guest or create an account. An account is optional. It lets you see your order history, save delivery addresses and check out faster. Creating an account is free.

You can delete your account at any time from your account settings. Deleting the account removes your profile and saved addresses. Orders you already paid for stay in our records for as long as tax law requires (see the [privacy policy](/privacy)).

## 4. Placing an order

1. Add products to your cart.
2. Go to checkout and enter your email address and delivery address.
3. Read these terms and the [privacy policy](/privacy), and tick the box to accept the terms.
4. Press Pay. You are taken to the secure payment page of our payment provider, Stripe, where you enter your card details.

The contract of sale is formed when your payment succeeds. Until then, you have not placed a binding order and we have not accepted one. Right after a successful payment we send you an order confirmation email with the order details, the version of these terms that applies, and links to these terms, the returns policy and the privacy policy.

## 5. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in {{store.currency}} and include VAT. The delivery fee is shown in the cart and at checkout before you pay, and is part of the total you pay. The price that applies is the one shown at checkout when you press Pay.

You pay by card on Stripe's hosted payment page. We never see or store your full card details. If the payment fails or you leave the payment page, no order is placed and your cart stays saved.

## 9. Complaints about faulty goods

We are responsible for delivering goods that conform to the contract. If the goods you receive are faulty or not as described, you have rights under the statutory warranty for non conforming goods (in Polish law, "rękojmia" or the rules on conformity of goods with the contract).

To file a complaint, write to {{seller.email}} or to our postal address above. Please include:

- your order number,
- a description of the problem and when you noticed it,
- what you would like us to do: repair, replace, reduce the price, or, where the law allows, end the contract and refund you.

Photos help us handle the complaint faster. We answer every complaint within 14 days of receiving it. If we do not answer within 14 days, the complaint counts as accepted.

## 10. Out of court dispute resolution

If we cannot settle a dispute together, you can use out of court ways to resolve it. In Poland these include the consumer ombudsman of your district or city (powiatowy or miejski rzecznik konsumentów) and the Trade Inspection (Inspekcja Handlowa). The European Commission's online dispute resolution information is available at [https://consumer-redress.ec.europa.eu](https://consumer-redress.ec.europa.eu).

Using these options is voluntary for both sides.
